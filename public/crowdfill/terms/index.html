1. What Crowdfill is

Crowdfill is a free browser extension that generates responses for Google Forms and, when you tell it to, submits them. It is meant for people who build and own forms: to test them, to load-test them, to produce demo and sample data, and to show what results look like when respondents lean a certain way.
