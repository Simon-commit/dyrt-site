About

dyrt is the studio name of Simon, an independent developer. The work ranges from browser extensions to applications, and follows three rules.

  • Open by default

    Source code is published wherever possible. RoLens is public under the MIT licence, so anyone can read exactly what it does.

  • Privacy first

    No analytics and no tracking. This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties.

  • Quiet interfaces

    Software should show what matters, where it matters, and otherwise stay out of the way.
