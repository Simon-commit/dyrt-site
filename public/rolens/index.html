RoLens

Live Rolimon's and RoUtility values, placed directly inside Roblox. Values, RAP, demand and USD estimates appear on trades, item pages and inventories, exactly where decisions are made.

View source Features

Features

Values in place

Every item in a trade carries its value and a USD estimate, with offer totals and the difference between both sides.

Trade previews

Every trades list, from Inbound to Inactive, shows what each offer is worth before it is opened, and flags items that have no value yet.

Inbound trade alerts

New inbound trades can reach Discord, the ntfy app or a desktop notification, filtered by value or gain. Only the trader's name, the items and their values are sent.

Outbound trade tools

RoLens finds outbound trades that can no longer complete and cancels them one at a time, only after you review and confirm the exact list.

Trade proofs

A completed trade becomes a shareable image with both sides, every value and the net result, drawn entirely on your device.

Nothing without your say

RoLens never sends, accepts or counters a trade and never acts on its own. Every request it makes is listed in the source.
